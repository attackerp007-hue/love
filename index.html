Tumi amar kache shudhu ekjon manush na. Tumi amar obbash, amar hasir karon, ar mon kharap hole jar kotha shobar age mone pore.

Shono bow, tomar upor amar rag hoyeche, obhiman hoyeche. Tumi jokhon amar sathe olpo kichu kotha bole edik-odik chole jao, tarpor offline hoye jao, tokhon amar mon ta kharap hoy. Ami jani tomar kaj thakte pare, nijer shomoy lagte pare. Ami sheta bujhte chai.

Kintu amar-o to mon ache. Tomar sathe aro kichukhon kotha bolte iccha kore, tomar din kemon gelo shunte iccha kore. Tomar ekta chotto message, ekta ador kore bola kotha amar puro din ta valo kore dite pare.

Rag kore ami jokhon bollam “ami gelam”, tokhon mone mone chaisilam tumi amake ektu atkao. Tumi jokhon “tomar iccha” bolle, tokhon khub koshto peyechi. Mone hoyechilo amar chole jawa niye tomar hoyto kichui jay ashe na. Hoyto ami tomar kache amar moner kotha thik moto bojhate parini.

Ami tomake dosh dite chai na. Amar-e vul chilo. Rag-er mathay emon kotha bola uchit hoyni. Sorry bow, amar kothay jodi tomar mon kharap hoye thake.

Ami chai na amader majhe rag-obhimaan jome thakuk. Ami chai amra dujon dujoner kotha bujhi, mon kharap hole chup kore dure na giye kotha boli. Tumi amar kache emn ekjon manus ja ami bole bujaite parbo nah, khub beshi vlobashi tomai.

Ekta kotha mone rekho—ami rag korte pari, obhiman korte pari, kokhono chup hoye jete pari; kintu tar mane ei na je tomar theke dure thakbo tomr kase ami here gesi amr ego amr selfrespect amr dignity amr sartthoportotta. Shob rag-er arale ekta manush ache, je tomake harate chay na, shudhu tomar chai ar kisu chaowa paoya nei tar.
